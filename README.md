# 📊 Finance Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Finance Sales Performance Dashboard** built in **Power BI**. The goal was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor financial performance, compare sales against budgets, evaluate profitability, and analyze invoice trends across different regions and sales representatives.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

This dashboard answers the following business questions:

1. What are the total Revenue, Cost, and Profit by Region?
2. What are the total Revenue, Cost, and Profit by Quarter?
3. Which Product Categories generate the highest Profit Margin?
4. How does Actual Sales compare to Budget across Regions?
5. How does Actual Sales compare to Budget by Sales Representative?
6. What are the Revenue and Profit trends over time (2023–2025)?
7. What is the distribution of Invoice Status (Paid, Pending, Overdue, and Cancelled)?
8. How does Invoice Status vary by Region?
9. How does Invoice Status vary by Payment Method?
